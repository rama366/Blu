Website terms

This website provides general freight information, planning tools, carrier links, and enquiry forms. Calculators, route suggestions, and transit information are indicative only. They are not a booking, carrier schedule, final quote, or promise of delivery time.

A shipment is accepted only when the parties agree to a written quote or service contract. That document should identify the route, services, charges, applicable duties or third-party costs, cancellation terms, and other shipment-specific conditions. Confirm cargo descriptions, dimensions, weights, permits, and handling requirements before booking.

External carrier and service links are operated by third parties. Their content, tracking data, terms, and privacy practices are controlled by those providers.

Payments, cancellations, and refunds

This website does not accept or process payments. Before booking a shipment, request a written quote that states the price, any deposit, other applicable charges, cancellation conditions, and refund terms. The terms in the accepted quote or service contract govern that shipment.

For a question about charges or a refund on an existing shipment, contact info@bluescompany.site and include your quote or shipment reference. Do not send payment-card details by email or through the quote form.

Privacy and data requests

The shipment planner works in your browser and does not store planner entries. Quote form submissions include the details you provide and are processed by Web3Forms to deliver your enquiry. See the Web3Forms privacy policy for its current handling and retention practices.

Customer accounts, marketplace listings, and inquiries are stored by Supabase to provide sign-in and the Truck Exchange. Listings are visible to signed-in members; inquiry contact details and messages are limited to the sender and listing owner under the site’s access rules. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Use your contact details only for the enquiry or account you intend. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, identity documents, or another person’s sensitive information. The account portal is intended for adults; do not create an account on behalf of a child or submit children’s personal data.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with an enquiry or account, email info@bluescompany.site from the address associated with the request. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Cookies and browser storage

The site currently has no Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or analytics cookies enabled. It uses local storage for the selected display theme and Supabase sign-in session, and session storage to remember dismissing this notice for the current tab. These are used to operate requested site functions. Google Fonts, Supabase, and Web3Forms are third-party services; review their policies for their own network and storage practices.

You can clear site storage in your browser settings. Clearing the Supabase session signs you out; clearing the theme preference restores the default appearance, and clearing session storage shows this notice again.

For shipment-specific terms, ask for a written quote before booking. Contact Blues Company.
